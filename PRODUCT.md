# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Students and early-career job seekers preparing for placements and entry-level interviews. They need a low-pressure way to practise before a real interview, understand how their answers land, and return to their progress over time.

## Product Purpose

AI Interview is an interview-practice web app. Users register, choose an interview category, answer dynamic questions, receive Google Gemini AI scoring and feedback, and review saved interview-session history. Success means users can complete focused practice sessions, understand concrete next steps for improvement, and build confidence for real interviews.

## Positioning

The product combines configurable mock-interview sessions, answer-by-answer Gemini coaching, model answers, self-assessment, and persistent progress history in one student-focused practice flow.

## Operating Context

Users practise HR/general, technical web-development, and behavioural questions. They configure category, difficulty, question count, and a per-question time limit, then complete timed written responses. After each answer, the app shows an AI score, feedback, strengths, and areas to improve. Completed sessions and answers are stored for later review.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Keep the working PHP backend, XAMPP Apache/MySQL deployment, MySQL session history, and Google Gemini evaluation integration intact.
- Keep user registration/login, dynamic randomized questions, model answers, tips, self-ratings, timers, and saved-session details intact.
- Existing pages are the home, login/register, interview setup, live interview, results, and history surfaces.
- The interface must work well on mobile and desktop.

## Brand Commitments

The interface should be focused, encouraging, accessible, polished, and consistent. Preserve the AI Interview product identity and the PBL project’s honest educational purpose.

## Evidence on Hand

- Working HTML/CSS/JavaScript frontend pages and PHP APIs in this project.
- MySQL-backed users, questions, interview sessions, and interview responses.
- Real Gemini feedback shown for each submitted answer.
- No approved external testimonials, customer claims, performance benchmarks, or brand assets are on hand; future UI work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Make the next practice action obvious and low-friction.
2. Turn AI feedback into clear, constructive coaching rather than a judgment.
3. Make progress and history useful evidence of improvement.
4. Keep the interview experience calm enough for learners under pressure.
5. Preserve the functional learning flow while improving clarity and visual consistency.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard-friendly, legible, responsive interaction and clear feedback for learners using mobile or desktop devices.
