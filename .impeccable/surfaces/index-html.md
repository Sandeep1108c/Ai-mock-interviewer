## Direction contract

THESIS: AI Interview is a focused dark workspace for practising one answer at a time, not a colorful AI dashboard or motivational landing page.

OWN-WORLD: Strictly monochrome surfaces: near-black base, charcoal panels, white copy, and neutral-gray borders and secondary text. Thin rules, disciplined spacing, and rectangular-to-gently-rounded controls create a calm documentation and editor vocabulary.

STORY: A learner understands the interview workflow immediately, starts a tailored practice session without friction, writes an answer in a distraction-free editor, and reads Gemini feedback as clear coaching they can act on.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A restrained header anchors the page. The hero makes the next practice action clear, while a live workspace preview demonstrates the question, answer field, timer, and coaching feedback. The primary action stays visually explicit without introducing color.

FORM: Dark knowledge workspace, candidate 7 of the grounded direction set, seed key 32eec4dd. Signature interaction: controls lift only slightly and progress or feedback states reveal through a short opacity-and-translate transition, respecting reduced-motion preferences.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
